# Ryesoma — Support

Ryesoma is made by Weft Witch Labs.

There is no account and no sign-in. The app is yours, on your phone, and it works offline.

**Contact: [support@weftwitchlabs.com](mailto:support@weftwitchlabs.com)**

When something has gone wrong, the most useful thing you can send is the build stamp at the bottom
of **Settings** — a short code and a date — along with what you were doing.

---

## Getting started

### Everything works offline, from the first launch

The food database — millions of foods, barcodes and micronutrients — is inside the app. There is
nothing to download and nothing to wait for: searching and scanning work on a phone that has never
had a network connection. You can also add a food of your own by hand at any time.

### Adding a food that is not in the database

Search for it; if it is not there, the search offers to add it. Enter what the label says — the form
can take the numbers **per serving** exactly as printed and convert them for you, as long as you give
it the serving size in grams.

You can also photograph a product: **Pantry → Photograph a product**. Type a name, take whatever the
package shows — the nutrition panel, the ingredients, the barcode — and finish it later from the
queue underneath. The photographs are saved the moment you take them.

### Scanning a barcode that finds nothing

Not every product is in the database. Add it once as your own food with that barcode, and every
future scan of it will find it straight away.

## Your data

### Where it is

On your device. There is no server and no account. See the privacy policy for the detail.

### Backing it up

**Settings → Import & export → Complete backup.** You get one file with your whole history in it.
Tick the box to include your photographs and videos as well — the file is much larger, and it is the
only copy of those pictures that exists anywhere else.

You can set a passphrase, which encrypts the backup. Nothing can open it without that passphrase,
including us — write it down somewhere that is not the phone. (A backup with photographs included
cannot be encrypted: it is too large to do in one piece on a phone.)

Keep it somewhere you will still have it if the phone is lost.

### A copy you can open in Excel

The same screen offers a **readable copy** — one spreadsheet with a sheet per table, or plain CSV
files. Excel, Numbers, LibreOffice and anything else open these directly, so this is the one to send
to someone or look at yourself. It can go in a password-protected zip if you want.

It does **not** restore. Use the complete backup for that.

### Restoring

The same screen. Choose the backup file; the app shows you what is in it and whether its integrity
seal still holds **before** anything is merged. Restoring merges — it does not wipe what is already
there.

### Moving to a new phone

Restore a backup made from the old one. If it included your photographs, they come back with it.

### Erasing everything

**Settings → Erase everything and start fresh.** It removes your data, your photographs, and any
stored sync credentials. Deleting the app does the same. Do this before you hand a phone on.

## Sharing a pantry

### What it is

Two or more devices keeping one pantry in step, through storage you already own. Nothing goes
through us.

### Setting it up

Pick where the shared file lives, set a passphrase, and share both with the other device. The
passphrase is not recoverable — if it is lost, the file cannot be read by anyone, including us. Keep
it somewhere safe.

### "The shared file could not be read yet"

Usually iCloud has not finished fetching the file onto this device. Nothing was changed. Wait a
moment and sync again.

### Someone else's change replaced mine

Quantities are merged rather than overwritten, and a recipe that two people edited keeps both — the
displaced version is kept as a copy rather than thrown away. If something looks wrong, check the
rescue tray before assuming it is gone.

## Common questions

**Do I need an internet connection?** Only for food recalls, shared pantries and importing a recipe
from a web address, and all three are optional. Food search, barcode scanning, logging, recipes, photographs and everything else work
with no connection at all.

**Does it read Apple Health?** Only if you connect it, in Settings. It reads heart rate, steps,
energy, sleep and workouts, never writes anything back, and keeps what it reads on your phone.

**Why does it want the microphone?** Only because a video recorded for a recipe step has sound. It
is never used otherwise.

**Why does it want my photo library?** Only when you choose an existing picture. Photographs you take
in the app stay in the app and are not written to your camera roll.

**Do the photos I add carry my location?** No. Location and other metadata are removed from
photographs the app stores.

**Can I use it on more than one device?** Yes, through a shared pantry or a shared cookbook, or by
restoring a backup.

## Reporting a problem

Send the build stamp from the bottom of Settings, what you were doing, and what happened instead.
If it involves a specific recipe, food or photo, say which — nothing about your data reaches us
otherwise, so a description is all there is to go on.
